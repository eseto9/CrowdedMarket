Setting up the stalls…

Run a stall on a busy market street. Beat each day’s score target, build up your mult, and see how far your run goes.

  1. 🎯 Each day is 75 seconds with a score target. Beat it to go on; miss it and the run is over. Every third day is a big one.
  2. 🧺 Stand at your stall: everyone in your ring gets an offer. Every sale scores price × mult, and your mult grows with every sale in a row.
  3. 🏃 Click a supplier to send your runner for stock. Lose a customer to a rival and your streak ends.
  4. 🫳 Customers on their way to a rival can be stolen: click them for a coin less.
  5. 🛍️ Between days, spend your coins in the shop on charms, staff and upgrades.
