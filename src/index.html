Setting up the stalls…

Run a stall on Market Street. Stock up, price it right and have the most coins when the closing bell rings.

  1. 🏃 Walk with WASD, dash with Space, or just click where to go.
  2. 🥖 Suppliers ring the market. Stand in a yellow ring and press E (or click it) to buy stock.
  3. 🧺 Take it back to your stall and put it on the shelf.
  4. 🗣️ Customers show what they want and the most they’ll spend. Stand at your stall and you sell to anyone in your ring; the cheapest offer wins.
  5. 🏷️ Press P at your stall to set prices. Blue bubbles are shopping lists: sell the whole list for a bonus.
  6. 🪙 Three days make a market week. Coins carry over, and you can buy upgrades between days. Leftover stock is worth nothing!
